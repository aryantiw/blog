Hypocrisy of Mind

On wanting connection while craving detachment, and the emotional intimacy hidden inside that contradiction.

By Aryan TiwariJune 11, 2023
In this note#relationships#detachment#feelings

I rant to my friends about not having any girlfriends. But I do not know why I even want one. I seek someone for me but still crave detachment from all worldly matters. One moment I desperately want someone in my life to make it better; the very next moment I feel like I want no one and can do it myself.

This duality of thoughts, this irony of mind, is difficult to explain. I come from a culture that follows non-duality of nature, but my very mind and my own thoughts oppose that tradition.

When I look into it, I find that I may not be over my previous encounters and the time spent in that territory. It is that which messes up my mind, or those thoughts that still make me crave these things. One thing is clear: I do not want physical intimacy to be everything. What I seek is emotional intimacy.

One that understands me, my thoughts, and my feelings. An old-fashioned guy with bare minimum communication skills is a perfect combination for most unsuccessful ventures across the territory of relationships.

But still, why that hypocrisy of mind? Why does it want what it wants? If it needs solace in a lover's arms, why does it also want to detach from all the material things and earthly matters? I cannot understand my mind and its hypocrisy, but it is what it is. That is how it works, and in the end everything is for the betterment.
