Magic, Science, Art and Divine

On miracles, Eureka moments, poetry, science, and the strange places where wonder begins.

By Aryan TiwariMay 27, 2023
In this note#science#art#wonder

What is miracle or magic in life? Do they really happen, or have they long vanished from this world? Many people claim that something occurs which gives them a second chance or a better chance at life. I do not know about these things. I am a believer, but do higher powers intervene in our lives? I doubt it.

Some people find art magical, some science. Whatever the domain, the effect is the same: a life-changing experience. Some find inspiration in science. A theorem can give them goosebumps and make them jump out of their bath tubs, like Archimedes running through the streets after his Eureka moment.

Others find it in art and poetry. Couplets about love make me believe in love once again, and motivational ones make my blood pump heavily, making me think I can conquer the world. Poetry can make you believe things, make you do things, and if you give your heart to it, perhaps your life too will change.

But what does this have to do with divinity? Does it interfere with our plans, or is there something in art and science that connects to the divine? I do not know. I might never know. Science has its own methods for finding the divine, searching for the God Particle. Art has its own praises for the divine in portraits, poems, and stories.

I study science, love art and history, believe in something higher, and remain skeptical about magic. So many opposites together, but somehow it works.
