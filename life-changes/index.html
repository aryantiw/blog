Life Changes

A poem about moving away from home, growing older, and searching for meaning through change.

By Aryan TiwariApril 4, 2023
In this note#poetry#change#home

The same monotonous life day in and day out

The nights are cool and breezy, days warm and cosy

But something is still missing

Living far from home, giving the chills even in the warmest days

The new life reminds me of the olden ways

The days passed by but something remained the same

You guessed it right, it was your name

Started off with the feeling that life is the same

Now found the meaning that it has continuously changed

The change is only constant, they say

But is it necessary, if I may?

The days before were colourful, now they are shades of grey

When we used to play barefoot without any worries

Today not a day passes without any worry

The future, no one has seen. Will it be colourful as the olden days?

With optimistic hopes we move ahead

To find the meaning and lead

The world has many, find your way

History repeats itself, they say.
