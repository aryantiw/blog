Humans: Past, Present & Future

A thought experiment about the ruling species on Earth, our tools, our divisions, and one human family.

By Aryan TiwariOctober 20, 2021
In this note#humans#evolution#unity

Humans are the ruling species on planet Earth. We are not the strongest or tallest among the species, nor do we have natural powers such as poison, sharp teeth, or claws to protect ourselves.

We succeeded through evolution and by using our brains to invent what we lacked: sharp tools from rocks, fire to keep predators away, and ways to cook food. But the tools originally made for protection turned toward fellow humans. We divided ourselves into tribes, ethnicities, races, castes, and colours.

Imagine yourself as an alien on a spaceship searching for life in the universe. You land on Earth and find the ruling species to be five feet tall, standing on two limbs and using two others for tasks. Some are tall, some short, some fat, some have black skin, some white. That is it. They are still the same beings and the same species.

The alien would not divide them into countries. It would simply say: this is the species found on every corner of this planet. This is the ruling species.

Why cannot we become one species, see each other as our own, and rise above differences of race, ethnicity, colour, creed, or religion? Why not see the whole Earth and humankind as one family, as the ancient Indian sages said: “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam.”
