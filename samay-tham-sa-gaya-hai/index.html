Samay Tham Sa Gaya Hai

A Hindi poem about a night that moves forward while time seems to stand still.

By Aryan TiwariMarch 29, 2023
In this note#hindi#poetry#time

Dur dur tak

Raat ki kaalima dikh rahi hai

Chhoti chhoti roshni ki boond dikh rahi hai

Jaise kudrat me mandrate jugnu ho

Thal aur aakash me antar jaan nahi padta

Raat ki kaalima chhayi hui hai

Kheton ki fasal leh laha rahi hai

Kuch mazdoor kaam kar rahe hain

Kuch wapas ja rahe hain

Dur kahin pe sadak malum padti hai

Uspe se paar hoti roshni ki boondein jeevan ki gati ka ehsaas karati hain

Par aisa lagta hai ki samay tham sa gaya hai

Chaaron or log apne karyon se vida le rahe hain. Sabhi apne aap ko ratri ke aagosh me khone ko tayyar hain.

Par kuch fir bhi jaagenge, koi apne premi ke liye, koi apne bhavishya ke liye. Sabka jeevan aage badhta rahega, par samay tham sa gaya hai.

— Aryan
