To life ka maksad kya hai? Hum koi cheez kyu karte hain? Usko karne ke peeche konse kaaran hote hain? Kya shaktiyan hoti hain jo hame motivated rakhti hain? Aur jab nahi rakh paati, to log aise sawal uthate hain: maksad kya hai? Kyu kar raha hu? Kiske liye kar raha hu?

Kya rakha hai mere liye at the end of this line of work? Bas ye sawal uthte hain, inke jawab nahi milte. Ya milenge, mai bas kuch jaldi hi puchh raha hu, dhundh raha hu, to kya mujhe jaldi mil jaane chahiye? Kabir ne kaha tha, "Dheere dheere re mana, dheere sab kuch hoye, maali seenche sau ghada, ritu aaye phal hoye."

Mera abhi season nahi aaya hai jisme mujhe ye answers mil jaye. Need to wait for some time to find the answers. Or, on a side note, am I even asking the right question? What if the question itself is wrong?

My consciousness is asking why it is conscious. What the hell? What is this level of being alive? Am I thinking too much or am I levelling up my consciousness? Whatever it is, I know only one thing: I am overwhelmed with these feelings. Love, betrayal, sadness, happiness, everything all at once.

What if I do not care about all this and just do whatever has to be done? Stop the bad habits and inculcate some good ones. Relax for one month, prepare like a warrior, go into exile, and come back at your peak intellect and physique. Then let the world see who you are.
