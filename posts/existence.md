Why are we here? We do not have any place in this universe. What are we? Why are we? Why do we live here, on this planet? Why are we alive?

We are no different from animals, living our lives mindlessly, not questioning our existence, performing our day-to-day jobs in a periodic manner. We never think about breaking the chain and asking what we are living for.

Is it the worldly pleasure we seek, or do we seek something else? The materialistic pleasure that we seek lasts only for a small moment. Then what is the source of eternal joy? What is the meaning of life? Why are we alive?

Pondering over these questions makes me wonder if even the gods know what the meaning of life is. As they too came after creation, did creation happen by their will? Did they know that creation happened? Who truly knows how this universe arose? Can even the god sitting on the highest plane grasp it, or is creation beyond even the highest sense of consciousness?
