The brand new space telescope, the James Webb Telescope, sent its first-ever captured images of the universe on 12 July. It allowed us to see new, untouched, unseen corners of the universe that were not available from its predecessor, the Hubble Telescope.

Every new image is a reminder that the universe does not become smaller when we understand more of it. It becomes more intimate, more astonishing, and more difficult to finish asking questions about.
