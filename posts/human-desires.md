The sole existence is the cause of suffering. The human nature of desiring is what makes us enter the loop of suffering, as the Buddha said: desire is suffering. But he did not say what the cause of desire is, or why we desire something.

According to me, the cause of desire is existence itself. If someone exists, they will desire something which will cause them suffering. So am I saying we should die to end suffering and desire? No. We should not die to break free from the loop. There are many paths to attain a blissful life without being bothered by these questions.

Existence is for finding the meaning of life, as many thinkers and philosophers before us have pondered. Consider a tiger's life: hunting, eating, mating. That is enough. As we evolved, our brains and thinking became more complex. We started asking questions about life, God, and existence, and we are still searching for answers.

In my opinion, do your job honestly, whatever it may be. If you are a student or a corporate worker, give your hundred percent to whatever you do. It makes your mind calm and improves your efficiency.
